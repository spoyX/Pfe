Our History, Mission and Vision

The Canada-Tunisia Chamber of Commerce (CTCC) was founded to promote economic and cultural exchanges between Canada and Tunisia. Our mission is to support businesses in their international development while strengthening bilateral relations. We believe in fruitful collaboration that benefits our members and partners.

FAQ

Find answers to the most frequently asked questions about our services and memberships.

To become a member, you must complete a membership application form available on our website. Once your application is submitted, our team will contact you to finalize your registration. Join us to enjoy numerous benefits.

We offer various services, including business accompaniment, event organization, and international trade advice. Each service is designed to meet the specific needs of our members. Feel free to explore our Services section for more details.

You can participate in our events by registering through our website. We regularly organize seminars, workshops, and business meetings. Be sure to check our event calendar to stay updated.

Our office is located in Tunis, but we collaborate with partners in Canada. Feel free to contact us for more information about our offices and activities. We're here to assist you with your inquiries.

You can contact us through our online form or by email. We strive to respond to all inquiries as quickly as possible. Don't hesitate to reach out with your questions or concerns.
