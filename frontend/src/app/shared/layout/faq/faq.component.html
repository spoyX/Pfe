Basic Questions
1. What is the CCCT?

The CCCT is dedicated to promoting economic and cultural exchanges between Canada and Tunisia.

2. How do I join the CCCT?

You can join by completing the registration form, verifying your email, and paying your membership fee online.

3. What are the benefits of membership?

Members gain access to exclusive events, networking opportunities, business resources, and more.
