Our History, Mission and Vision

The Canada-Tunisia Chamber of Commerce (CTCC) was founded to promote economic and cultural exchanges between Canada and Tunisia.

Our Core Values

At the heart of the CCCT lies a strong belief in integrity, inclusivity, and innovation. We encourage responsible commerce that respects both cultures and prioritizes long-term, sustainable partnerships.

Every decision we take is driven by a commitment to fairness and mutual benefit for all stakeholders.

Our Strategic Objectives

We aim to provide structured support to businesses at every stage of their international journey — from market entry analysis to on-the-ground networking and guidance.

By creating bridges between institutions and entrepreneurs, we serve as a key facilitator for cross-border cooperation.

Our Impact

Over the years, CTCC has enabled dozens of successful partnerships and business expansions across both continents. Our members have gained access to exclusive events, insightful market data, and reliable contacts within both ecosystems.

We continue to grow and adapt, reflecting the evolving needs of Canadian and Tunisian enterprises.
